## dataset A: lab reruns

The lab engine's result on this dataset is the outcome that 19 of the 20 reruns above share, not the one saved reconstruction. The saved reconstruction sits with a rarer outcome that one rerun reaches. Distances are the mutual error on the complex whole array after shift, phase, twin and scale.

- 19 of the 20 reruns lie 0.05-0.09 from the mean of the other 19.
- One rerun, seed 16, lies 0.235 from the mean of the others, as far as the saved reconstruction does. It is also the rerun closest to the saved reconstruction, at 0.127; the other 19 lie 0.229-0.240 from it (median 0.233).
- Seed 16 and the saved run differ from the other reruns in the same direction. Seed 16 has the highest engine chi of the 20 (0.1047, against a median of 0.0966; saved run 0.0990). It also has the smallest support (5007 voxels, against a median of 5545; saved run 5279) and the worst Fourier residual (0.324, against 0.309-0.311 for the others; saved run 0.315 cut to its support).
- The saved run used the same recipe as the reruns, and by the engine's chi it converged like them.

So the 0.235 between the saved reconstruction and every rerun, QoraPhase's included, is not a difference between the engines. The saved reconstruction differs from the mean of the 20 reruns by 0.215:

- 65% of the difference lies 8-16 px from the centre of the pattern, where the data have 1-3 counts per voxel. In each q shell below 16 px the two fit the data equally well, to within 0.004.
- In real space 78% lies inside the support, 21% at its boundary (one voxel either side) and none outside.
- Most of it is amplitude: the amplitudes alone differ by 0.168.

Not established: the saved run's random start (its seed was not saved), and the GPU and MATLAB version it ran on. With one rerun of 20, how often the rarer outcome occurs is not known. Physics check needed: whether the two outcomes are physically different is left to the owner.
